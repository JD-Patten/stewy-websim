Policies: three walkers, three directions each by Stewy's 3-fold symmetry: the smoothest one, now on the robot (1.75 s cycle, 43 mm/s in Isaac Lab, plate 3.2 m/s² rms), the 1.25 s-cycle walker (51 mm/s, plate 4.5 m/s²) and the fast one it replaced (62 mm/s, plate 7.7 m/s²), an any-direction walker from 2026-09-29 (one 60° wedge, mirrored and rotated to all 12 directions of a 40 mm/s command; in Isaac Lab it walks about 25 mm/s and walks the six in-between directions along a neighbouring one), a newer any-direction walker trained with no plate penalty (2026-09-30, 40 mm/s in Isaac Lab but plate 7.7 m/s², and it drifts in yaw), the three-walker set (2026-09-30): each of the 12 directions is walked by one of three single-direction walkers, rotated or mirrored: A = the robot's walker (toward the head, 30°, 150°), B = the firmware gait played backwards (away from the head, −30°, −150°; 42.7 mm/s, plate 4.1 m/s²) and C = the firmware gait steered to −60° (0°, ±60°, ±120°, 180°; 42.3 mm/s, plate 2.5 m/s², lands about 5° short), plus five turn-in-place policies (the other way by mirror symmetry): the one now on the robot, trained for calm legs (42 °/s in Isaac Lab, legs 28 rad/s² rms, plate 2.1 m/s²), the first robot turner (48 °/s, yaw rate wobbles ±24 °/s, plate 3.9 m/s²) and three smoother retrains from 2026-09-29: steady rate (wobble ±10 °/s), calm plate (2.6 m/s²) and both (±14 °/s, 2.8 m/s²). Servos use the MG90S model measured on the bench. The terrain is new to them: every policy was trained on flat ground, blind, so this shows how far that carries.
